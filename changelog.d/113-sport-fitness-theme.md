- A new Sport & Fitness theme template separates sport and bike spend
  from Health & Fitness (#113), which mixed gyms and sport in with
  pharmacy and general medical. It adds merchant rules for bike shops
  and bike parks, which were usually filed as Shopping or Transportation
  and so never reached a sport-flavoured subcategory in the first
  place. Personal training has no guessed merchant; a Personal Training
  subcategory rule picks one up automatically once a lookup identifies
  it, or you can add a merchant rule yourself from the theme editor.
  Existing themes, including Health & Fitness, are untouched.
